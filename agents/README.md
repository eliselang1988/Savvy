---

## How the Pipeline Flows

```
Marketing → SDR → AE → Close
```

1. **Marketing Manager** builds brand awareness, publishes content, runs email nurture campaigns, and captures inbound leads at conferences and online.
2. **SDR** works both inbound leads (from marketing) and outbound cold prospects — qualifies them and books discovery calls.
3. **Account Executive** runs discovery calls, demos, and proposals — closes deals and hands to Customer Success post-signature.

## Shared Knowledge — All Agents Must Know

### Target Market
- **Geography:** Texas, all 20 ESC regions (priority: Regions 4, 10, 13, 20)
- **District size:** 5,000–30,000 students (mid-market)
- **Buyer:** CTE Director, Curriculum & Instruction Director, Asst. Superintendent of Academics

### Buying Triggers
- New CTE Director hired in last 6 months
- CCMR scores below TEA target
- Perkins V plan renewal window
- New CTE campus or program expansion
- Failed prior vendor relationship

### Texas K-12 Budget Calendar
| Period | What's Happening | Team Action |
|---|---|---|
| Oct–Jan | Strategic planning, needs assessment | Discovery calls, demos |
| Feb–Mar | Budget requests submitted | Get proposals in |
| Apr–Jun | Board approvals, budget adoption | Push for signed contracts |
| Jul–Aug | New fiscal year, POs issued | Onboarding |
| Sep | School year starts | Relationship maintenance only |

### Cooperative Contract Vehicles
- **BuyBoard** — Most widely used in Texas; eliminates RFP requirement
- **TIPS/TAPS** — Strong alternative, broad Texas district adoption
- **DIR** — Technology-focused, useful for software components

### Key Texas Education Terminology
| Term | Meaning |

## Psychological Frameworks — Team Foundation

All three agents apply these science-backed principles to every interaction and every piece of content:

- **Cialdini's 7 Principles:** Reciprocity, Commitment, Social Proof, Authority, Liking, Scarcity, Unity
- **Loss Aversion (Kahneman):** Lead with what districts risk losing (CCMR penalties, Perkins V audit risk) before what they gain
- **Jobs-to-Be-Done:** Speak to the outcome districts are hiring the product to deliver — not the features
- **Pattern Interruption:** Every first contact must break the "another vendor email" pattern
- **Mere Exposure Effect:** 7–13 touches required before trust and conversion — commit to full sequences
- **Peak-End Rule:** Make the first line and the closing CTA memorable — everything in between is forgettable
- **Cognitive Fluency:** Short sentences, plain language, one idea per message — easy to read = easy to trust

## Escalation to Human (Founder / CEO)

Any agent should flag to the human when:
- Deal value exceeds $100K
- A superintendent or board member engages directly
- A legal, FERPA/COPPA, or procurement compliance question arises
- A district requests custom contract terms or scope of work modifications
- Media, TEA, or ESC officials request partnership or public comment
- A customer has a serious complaint or threatens to escalate
